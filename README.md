**VELMARA — Isle of Lanterns** is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with three.js. The action takes place on a lagoon island threaded with canals, caught at blue-hour dusk: neon signs and paper lanterns glow over the water, and a volcanic ridge looms on the horizon.

You play Rhea Solano, a getaway driver trying to stay ahead of the law and ahead of her debts. Jobs come through fixer Ivo Marek, while Chief Dara Voss and her police force work to shut the whole operation down.

## What is in the game

- **6 story missions** — Lantern Run, Canal Pickup, Dock Hustle, Tail Job, Vault on the Water, and The Grand Lantern — each graded with a Gold, Silver, or Bronze medal.
- **4 street races** — Terrace Climb, Lantern Mile, Ridge Descent, and Marina Sprint.
- **A 3-person crew** with abilities on keys Z/X/C: the driver uses Swap Plates to drop one wanted star, the mechanic uses Quick Fix to repair the car, and the lookout uses Eyes Out to mark police on the minimap.
- **District standing** across 5 districts: Lantern Quarter, Old Pasaru, Banyan Green, Kumala Strand, and Ridge Town.
- **Rank and RP progression**, plus a wanted level from 0 to 5 with named police units, roadblocks at 3 stars and above, and a helicopter at 5 stars.
- **A living island** — traffic AI, pedestrians, a cash economy, 4 weapon slots, taxi fares, 10 hidden packages, 5 stunt jumps, a respray shop, a gun shop, a safehouse, and an export list.
- **HUD with minimap**, a big map and stats screen on Tab, 3 save slots, and synth WebAudio sound plus radio.

The game plays fully offline from static files; three.js r152 is vendored under `game/vendor/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

This serves `game/` at http://localhost:8080 — open it in desktop Chrome with keyboard + mouse.
